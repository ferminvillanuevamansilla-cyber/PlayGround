HOW TO PLAY

🕹EXPLORE

Use the joystick or WASD. Drag the world to look around. Space jumps.

⛏MINE

Hold MINE, or left-click a block. Chop logs, dig stone, and gather materials.

🧱BUILD

Pick a block below, then tap PLACE or right-click. Build a tower, bridge or cosy hut.
